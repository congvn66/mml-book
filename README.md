# My Journey of Learning Mathematics for Artificial Intelligence

This repository documents a structured personal journey through mathematics for Machine Learning and Deep Learning. It contains solutions, derivations, and notes from textbooks, lecture notes, and online courses. All content is implemented in Jupyter Notebooks and LaTeX handbooks.

# Folder & Content Description

- **linear algebra** - My solutions to Chapters 2, 3, and 4 in *Mathematics for Machine Learning* (M. P. Deisenroth, A. A. Faisal, C. S. Ong).
- **vector calculus** - My solutions to Chapter 5 in *Mathematics for Machine Learning* (M. P. Deisenroth, A. A. Faisal, C. S. Ong).
- **probability** - My solutions for recitation problems and problem sets of *Probabilistic Systems Analysis and Applied Probability* (MIT OCW). *(Still working on it!)*
- **convex optimization** - A LaTeX handbook on mathematical optimization. It provides a concise foundation in basic convex optimization (convex sets/functions, separation theorems), Lagrange and Fenchel duality, and the steepest descent algorithm. The **primary focus** of this handbook is a deep dive into the **Mirror Descent** framework—covering its Bregman subproblem, dual-space updates, Fenchel-Young duality connections, Legendre potential conditions (H1–H4), and geometric/Majorization-Minimization interpretations.
